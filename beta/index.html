❗ Quiz Me!

Quiz Me! was a 1st party voice-forward trivia skill powered by Alexa's own Knowledge Graph. Players could test their knowledge across thousands of dynamically generated and engaging categories.

Alexa Skill · Featured at the 2019 Game Developers Conference

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Five-star review

"I really like this game. We play it on long drives — so traffic doesn't bother us as much. I love movie trivia and there are lots of variations in this game — as well as a lot of other types of categories. I only wish there was a list of the available categories somewhere. I figured out that I can say: 'Quiz me on movies from the 1930's' (or 40's, 50's, etc.) or you can ask to be quizzed on movies from specific directors or specific actors. In a couple of instances the skill had some issues: a few times it read the questions in Spanish instead of English and sometimes it says it does not have a category that I know it has because I've played it already. But the problems are not often."

📙 The Dictionary Game

The popular parlor game, now playable online with friends! Trick your friends into guessing your made-up definition of an obscure word, and see if you can spot the correct definition among the crowd. Players use their phones, tablets, or laptops to play together in real time. At least 2 players are required, but the more players join, the harder and crazier it becomes to get your friends to vote for your definition.
